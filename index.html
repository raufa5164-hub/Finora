ABOUT THE PROJECT

Finora – AI-Powered Personal Finance Manager

Empowering everyone to spend smart and save better.

Balancing a monthly budget while managing cash, UPI, and bank accounts can be challenging. Finora brings everyday personal finance tasks into one unified platform so everyone can track spending, plan budgets, and work toward savings goals with greater clarity.

Current release: v1.1.3

💳

Multi-Wallet Management

Organize Bank, Cash, UPI, and other payment methods in one dashboard.

📊

Tracking & Analytics

Review income, expenses, categories, and spending trends through visual summaries.

🎯

Budgets & Savings Goals

Set a savings target and optional category-wise spending limits.

🔎

Spending Insights

View rule-based spending indicators and estimated forecasts based on available transaction history.

📱

PWA & Offline Support

Use the installable web app with local data storage and cached app resources when offline.

☁️

Cloud Synchronization

Sign in with Google to synchronize personal finance data through Firebase services.

Our Vision & Impact

Finora aims to encourage financial awareness and responsible spending for everyone. Its goals align broadly with SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth), SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure), and SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production).
